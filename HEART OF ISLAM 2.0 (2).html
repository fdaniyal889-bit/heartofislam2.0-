About Us

Heart of Islam 2.0 shares Islamic reminders, Quranic lessons, Islamic knowledge and motivational content to encourage people to learn and practise Islam.

Our goal is simple: to help hearts return to Allah with sincerity, knowledge and hope. We strive to rely on the Quran and authentic sources, and we ask Allah to accept this effort.

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

“Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured.”

Quran 13:28
